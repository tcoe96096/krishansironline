Privacy Policy for Krishan Sir

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

At Krishan Sir, accessible from https://krishansir.online, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and students. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Krishan Sir and how we use it.

1. Consent

By using our website (https://krishansir.online), you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms and conditions.

2. Information We Collect

  • Voluntary Information: If you contact us via email, doubt-clearing forms, or feedback portals, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, class/grade, and message contents.
  • Online Tests & CBT Simulators: When students take online mock tests or quizzes, test scores and answers are processed to display instant results.
  • Technical Data: Standard non-personally identifiable log data (IP address, browser type, device info) is automatically collected by our hosting infrastructure.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our educational website (Classes 6 to 12 study material and tests).
  • Improve our HBSE/CBSE syllabus resources and CBT simulators.
  • Respond to academic queries and prevent spam or automated abuse.

4. Cookies and Google AdSense (DART Cookie)

krishansir.online uses cookies to store visitor preferences. Third-party vendors, including Google, use DART cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. External Educational Links

Our portal links to official boards (HBSE, SCERT Haryana, NCERT, CBSE). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

6. Children's & Student Privacy

Our platform is designed for school students (Classes 6 to 12). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 without parental guidance.
